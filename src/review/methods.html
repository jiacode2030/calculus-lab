Which tool do I use?

Most of the time lost on the exam goes into choosing a method. Learn the clues, then practice picking.

The intuition

Each problem type leaves clues. "Find the slope at a point" means a derivative. "Total change" or "area" means an integral. A 0/0 limit means factor, simplify or use L'Hôpital. "How fast is it changing" with two linked quantities means related rates. Name the method first; the algebra is the easy part.
