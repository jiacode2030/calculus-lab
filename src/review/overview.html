How to use a module

  1. Read The idea: the concept in everyday words.
  2. Play with the graph. Drag the points and move the sliders; the numbers underneath update as you go.
  3. Do Try this: guess first, then press Set it up for me and check.
  4. Work the Worked example one step at a time.
  5. Answer Check yourself, then do the practice problems.

Look for dotted words: tap them for a quick definition. Every simulation has a Coach that explains whatever control you just moved, and a Reset button.

Four ideas you'll use everywhere

  • Zoom in far enough and a curve looks like a line. That line's slope is the derivative.
  • A derivative is a rate. Always ask: rate of what, per what?
  • An integral adds up thin slices. Area, distance, volume and total change are all the same move.
  • The two are opposites. The Fundamental Theorem says so, and it is why antiderivatives work.
