Exam format

AP Calculus AB and BC · May 2027 exam · about 3 hours 10 minutes

Section I: Multiple choice
42 questions · 100 min · 50%
  • Part A: 29 questions, 62 min, no calculator
  • Part B: 13 questions, 38 min, graphing calculator required
Four answer choices each.
Section II: Free response
6 questions · 90 min · 50%
  • Part A: 2 questions, 30 min, graphing calculator required
  • Part B: 4 questions, 60 min, no calculator
Show your work: answers need the reasoning and units, not just a number.
How you take it
Hybrid digital. You answer multiple choice in the Bluebook app. Free-response questions appear in Bluebook, and you handwrite your answers in a paper booklet.
Calculator
Only in the calculator parts (Section I Part B and Section II Part A): an approved graphing calculator, or the Desmos calculator built into Bluebook. The other parts are no calculator.
Reference sheet
None. You need to know the derivatives, integrals and series yourself; this site's Reference button helps you learn them.
AB or BC
Same layout and timing. BC adds Units 9 and 10 (parametric, polar, vector functions and series) and a few more integration topics, and also reports an AB subscore.
What changed
For May 2027, multiple choice went from 45 questions in 105 minutes to 42 in 100. Free response and the course content are the same.

Checked October 23, 2026 against College Board's published format. Formats can change, so confirm on AP Central before your exam.
